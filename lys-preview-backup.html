The LYS Standard
Not paper. Not metal. Five thousand years of silk craft, reimagined for global collectors.
I

Silk Medium

Premium mulberry silk, micro-jet printed to reproduce every detail. The natural sheen shifts with light — a texture no paper or metal can match. Dissolvable backing enables dual-use as wall art or wearable silk.

II

Strictly Limited

Collector's Editions: 25–100 pieces, hand-embroidered numbering on reverse, certificate of authenticity. Sold out means gone forever — no reprints, no matter the demand. One NFT 1/1 per drop, auctioned separately.

III

Global Stage

Works enter physical exhibitions in Macau, Beijing, Taipei, Paris and beyond. Theme exhibitions tied to our 12 categories, plus solo shows for headlining artists. We are a bridge — Eastern silk art to the world.

For Artists Worldwide

We seek creators with real conviction — from East and West. Your work will be printed on premium silk, released to collectors worldwide, and given the chance to enter museum-grade exhibitions. You retain full copyright. We handle the rest.

01

Revenue Share

Standard Editions: 25–30% of sales revenue. Collector's Editions: 35–40%. NFT 1/1 auctions: 8–10% royalty, split artist/platform. We cover silk material, printing, logistics, and customer service.

02

Exhibition Exposure

Outstanding works are prioritized for physical exhibitions in Macau, Beijing, Taipei, Paris and beyond. Theme shows tied to our 12 categories, plus potential solo exhibitions. This is a stage for museum-grade audiences.

03

Collectible Value

Every work is strictly limited — 25–500 pieces, numbered and certified. Sold out and retired. Your work carries long-term appreciation potential in the secondary market, with NFT royalty for the 1/1 edition.

04

Creative Freedom

3-year exclusive license for silk print sales and exhibition usage. You retain full copyright of your work. No open registration — every artist goes through portfolio review to protect collection quality.

Founder & Creative Director

Sean Own

E-commerce Merchandiser · Cultural Entrepreneur

Sean Own is not a fashion designer — he is a merchandiser. For over a decade, he has specialized in taking cultural products and turning them into commercially successful goods, serving over 80 companies and generating over 100 million RMB in revenue through live commerce and e-commerce.

As founder of Long You Group, president of the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area Electronics Chamber of Commerce, and director of the Macau Long You Silk Culture and Art Center, Sean has long focused on two missions: AI industry implementation, and the international传播 of Chinese culture.

Featured on CGTN French channel's "Taiwanese in Macau" series, Sean shared his vision of building cultural bridges — bringing the excellence of Chinese culture to the world through Macau's unique position as a East-West cultural crossroads.
